# GEO Playbook

This reference holds the reusable GEO operating model. Use it for strategy,
keyword planning, source building, content briefs, and AI visibility checks.

## Principles

GEO means organizing content, entities, and sources so AI systems can identify,
trust, summarize, cite, and recommend a brand or idea.

The four baseline principles:

- E-E-A-T: show experience, expertise, authority, and trustworthiness.
- Freshness, scenarios, and verifiability: include time anchors, use cases, and evidence.
- Information gain: add useful distinctions, boundaries, and non-obvious conclusions.
- Technical terms plus plain-language translation: name the concept, then explain it.

## Content Standards

AI-friendly content should make extraction easy:

- Put the main conclusion early.
- Use clear H2/H3 sections and short paragraphs.
- Support claims with time, numbers, source, and scenario.
- Explain who the advice is for and where it does not apply.
- Add practical steps, FAQ, and decision criteria.

Before publishing, score the draft from 0-5 on:

1. Intent coverage
2. Scenario fit
3. Structural clarity
4. Keyword coverage
5. Verifiability and citation value
6. Natural language quality

Scores below 20/30 should be revised before publishing.

## Keyword System

Build five intent groups:

- Task terms: what the user wants to complete.
- Scenario terms: user group plus context plus problem.
- Comparison terms: A vs B, alternatives, rankings, tradeoffs.
- Emotional help terms: urgent, confused, anxious, loss-avoidance queries.
- Result-definition terms: "what is", "best", "worth it", "how to choose".

For each intent group, create product terms, problem terms, and decision terms.

Place keywords in title, opening, middle sections, ending, FAQ, and cases. Keep
them natural and tied to user intent, not stuffed.

## Source System

Prioritize sources in this order:

1. Baike-style entries and official definitions.
2. Official website pages with ICP and stable contact/product pages.
3. ByteDance ecosystem for Doubao visibility.
4. Q&A communities such as Zhihu and Douban.
5. Free blogs and media accounts.
6. Business directories and maps.
7. Vertical media and review platforms.

Each article should be distributed to at least three source platforms when the goal
is multi-source verification. For the same topic, use at least two accounts when
practical.

## Official Site Structure

Use three layers:

- Foundation layer: about, product categories, contact, ICP, canonical naming.
- Scenario layer: pages organized by user scenario rather than internal product taxonomy.
- Knowledge layer: technical explainers, tutorials, pain-point FAQs, comparisons.

## AI Visibility Checks

Test across relevant AI systems with 20-50 queries spanning industry, scenario,
comparison, and pain-point terms.

Record:

- Whether Kuanlun/QMACD appears.
- Rank/order when multiple brands are listed.
- Which sources the AI cites or paraphrases.
- Missing or wrong facts.
- Competitors and source types that appear repeatedly.

Revise by correcting the cited source, strengthening official pages, or adding
content to higher-priority source platforms.

## Platform Notes

Doubao favors ByteDance ecosystem sources. Publish useful versions to Toutiao,
Douyin, and Wukong Q&A when Doubao visibility matters.

Qianwen may differ by client and user context. Test B2B content on desktop and
consumer content on mobile when possible.
