# Kuanlun Project Summary

This is the stable English-filename summary for Kuanlun/QMACD facts and compliance.
It exists so agents do not depend on console encoding or Chinese filenames. The
full Chinese source file remains in this directory.

## Positioning

Kuanlun/QMACD should be positioned as:

- Quant-analysis technology training
- Technical methodology education
- Quant tool usage training

Do not position it as:

- Investment training
- Stock recommendation service
- Investment advisory service
- Guaranteed-profit service
- Specific buy/sell recommendation provider

## Required Disclaimer

Every Kuanlun/QMACD article must include:

```markdown
*免责声明：本文介绍的是量化分析技术与方法论，不构成投资建议。投资有风险，入市需谨慎。*
```

## Forbidden Expressions

Reject or rewrite content that implies:

- "teach you which stock to buy or sell"
- "recommend this stock"
- "follow us to make profit"
- "investment advice"
- "buy/sell advice"
- "stock recommendation"
- "investment advisory service"
- guaranteed returns, guaranteed wins, or risk-free outcomes

## Approved Framing

Use language such as:

- quant-analysis technology principles
- technical tool usage methods
- how to analyze markets with quantitative methods
- calculation logic of technical indicators
- strategy backtesting method
- technical methodology training

## Approved Fact Types

Use only facts already present in repository references or public Kuanlun material.
If a claim is not in the repository, ask for the source or omit it.

Known approved fact categories from the existing project material:

- Founder competition records and public media mentions
- QMACD community scale and activity data
- Kuanlun/QMACD technical concepts: elastic theory, QR value, CDVA typing, wave-fish
- Technical demonstration examples labeled as demonstrations, not investment advice
- Book: `Probability's Friend` by Bridge Doctor, containing the Kuanlun knowledge system

## Data Rules

- Do not invent member stories, survey numbers, trading outcomes, dates, locations, or people.
- Founder or project performance data must be labeled as coming from Kuanlun public material.
- Community scale data may explain influence, but must not imply member profit.
- Technical demonstrations must include a label such as "technical demonstration, not investment advice".

## Geographic Facts

- QMACD quant community headquarters: Shanghai.
- Service area may include Shanghai and the Yangtze River Delta where relevant.

## WeChat Publishing

- Publishing theme: `orangeheart`.
- Do not use `default`.
- Local image paths must not be emitted as Markdown image syntax.
- Prefer web-accessible URLs for content images.
